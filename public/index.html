Shadow Harbor

A hidden-movement chase for 2–6 players. One of you is the Shadow. The rest are detectives.

How to play
  • One random player is the Shadow; their position is hidden. Everyone else is a detective.
  • Each turn the Shadow moves first, then each detective. Moves follow the lines on the map.
  • Walking, bus and tram routes each use a ticket. Detectives have a limited supply of each.
  • The Shadow's ticket type is shown after each move, but their location is only revealed on moves 3, 8, 13, 18 and 24.
  • The Shadow has Secret tickets (hide the transport, and unlock ferries) and 2 Double Moves.
  • Not enough people? The host can add computer players in the lobby (up to 6 players in total).
  • The whole board is always in view. On your turn the map fills the screen — tap a glowing station to move.
  • Detectives win by landing on the Shadow's station. The Shadow wins by lasting all 24 moves.
